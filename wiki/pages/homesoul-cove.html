HomeSoul Cove

A 3D homebrew plaza backed by HomeSoulDB.

Features

  • Persistent player profiles with simple generated avatars
  • Claimable stands with short billboard copy
  • Catalog submissions routed through HomeSoulDB
  • Text chat and voice-room signaling metadata
  • Jam-ready exhibition metadata for booths

Core endpoints

POST /api/cove/presence
GET  /api/cove/state
POST /api/cove/chat
GET  /api/cove/voice/:standId
