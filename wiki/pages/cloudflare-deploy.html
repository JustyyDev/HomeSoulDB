Showcase Guide

Present projects in a way that visitors can understand in seconds.

Most people do not arrive on a site ready to study internal tooling. They arrive wanting to know what the engine is, what creators are building with it, and which projects or events are worth clicking first.

Visitor rule

Lead with identity, mood, a short pitch, and one strong image or stand concept. Do not bury the interesting part under process talk.

Showcase principle: the first ten seconds should tell a visitor what the project is, why it matters, and what kind of experience it promises.

What every showcase should communicate

Immediate identity

Name the project clearly and give it a visual hook or memorable theme line.

Playable focus

Tell people whether it is a mod, a full game, a prototype, or an art-heavy concept build.

Readable pitch

Billboard text and short blurbs should be tight enough to skim quickly in a catalog or on a stand.

Event context

If a project is part of a jam, say which event it belongs to and what category it fits.

Good showcase checklist

Project name
Short pitch line
Clear genre or format
Screenshot or stand concept
Why it stands out
Jam or release context
One call to action
