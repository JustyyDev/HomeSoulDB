Showcase Guide

Make the engine and its projects feel exciting to real visitors.

This guide is about presentation. Use it when writing project blurbs, curating the catalog, preparing HomeSoul Cove booths, or building pages that introduce people to the scene.

Main rule

Lead with the project, not the pipeline behind it.

A strong public-facing showcase needs

A clear identity

Name, theme, and tone should be visible immediately.

A short readable pitch

Visitors should understand the idea before they have to scroll.

A reason to click

Use one detail that sparks curiosity, whether that is music style, mechanics, art direction, or setting.

Context

Say whether the project is a jam submission, a long-term game, or a focused demo.
