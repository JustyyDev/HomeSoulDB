What SoulScorch Actually Is

A rhythm-first engine that is stretching into a bigger creative platform.

SoulScorch still carries the homebrew spirit. It is scrappy, creator-facing, and heavily mod-oriented. At the same time, the codebase already covers much more than a basic FNF fork. It ships scripting backends, chart and asset systems, editors, cutscenes, dialogues, shaders, replays, 3D experiments, and a community catalog layer.

This wiki is written to help people understand where the engine is today and where it can go next. The goal is not to sound corporate. The goal is to make the engine usable, understandable, and welcoming to people who want to build weird, stylish, or ambitious projects.

Roadmap Tracks

Mod-first workflow

Small asset packs, larger overhauls, and custom gameplay behaviors should all feel like native use cases, not awkward exceptions.

Game-scale projects

The engine needs documentation and tooling that support full original games built on the same base, including custom progression, hubs, and presentation.

Community visibility

HomeSoulDB and HomeSoul Cove are the discovery layer. They help people browse, test, showcase, and talk about projects inside the broader SoulScorch space.
